Email to Ronny

Материал пройден

You have received an email message from your English-speaking pen-friend Ronny:

From: Ronny@mail.uk

To: Russian_friend@ege.ru

Subject: Summer

…I am so happy that summer has come and we are going to have a long holiday. What’s the weather like in Russia in summer? What is your favourite season and why this one? What are your plans for the summer? My uncle Keith is coming to visit us next week...

Write an email to Ronny.

In your message:

  • answer his questions;
  • ask 3 questions about his uncle.

Write 100–140 words.

Remember the rules of email writing.

Показать ответСкрыть ответ

From: Russian_friend@ege.ru

To: Ronny@mail.uk

Subject: Re: Summer

Hi Ronny,

Thanks for your email! I'm glad to hear from you. Summer is my favourite season too!

You asked me about the weather in Russia. In summer it's usually warm and sunny, with temperatures around 25–30 degrees. Sometimes it rains, but the rain is warm and short.

My favourite season is summer because I have a lot of free time. I can swim, ride a bike and spend time with my friends outdoors.

This summer I'm going to visit my grandparents in the countryside and then go to the seaside with my family.

By the way, you mentioned your uncle Keith. How long is he going to stay with you? What does he do? Why is he coming to visit you?

Write back soon!

Alex
